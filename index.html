    Unleashing Profitability: The Labor–R&D Nexus in SaaS Tech Firms

    High-tech firms often prioritize expansion over profitability, which leaves them exposed in downturns. This study measures how labor and R&D spending affect profitability in SaaS companies, using fixed-effects and stochastic frontier analysis.

    DiVA · Blekinge Institute of Technology

    M.Sc. thesisSep 2017

    Design and Implementation of an Intrusion Detection System (IDS) for In-Vehicle Networks

    The CAN bus was designed without any security mechanisms. This thesis builds a lightweight, software-based IDS with timing and plausibility detectors, and tests it on a real in-vehicle network together with Volvo Cars. It caught malformed frames, implausible signals and denial-of-service attacks.
